Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Unified Gallery ("the app") is an Android gallery and backup app. This policy explains what data the app uses and how. In short: the app has no servers of its own, collects no analytics, and never sends your data to anyone other than Google, on your behalf, to your own Google Drive.

Data the app accesses

  • Photos and videos on your phone: to show them, and to back up the ones you choose.
  • Your Google account's basic profile (name, email address, profile picture), to show which accounts are connected.
  • Your Google Drive (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive): to list the photos, videos and files already in your Drive, upload backups to a "Unified Gallery Backup" folder, read each account's storage quota, and move to Drive's trash, or permanently delete, files only when you ask the app to.

How the data is used and stored

  • Everything happens on your phone. Files travel directly between your phone and Google's servers over HTTPS.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on your phone and are used only to talk to Google Drive for you.
  • The app keeps a small local record of which photos were backed up to which account, your albums, favourites and settings. This stays on your phone.
  • Items you put in the app's Trash or Locked folder are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs. The only data transfer is between your phone and Google, initiated by you.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Unified Gallery's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your control

  • Remove an account any time in the app (Settings → account → Remove); this also revokes the app's access.
  • You can also revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backups stay in your Google Drive until you delete them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact
