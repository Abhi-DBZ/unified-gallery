Unified Gallery

One gallery for the photos on your phone and in all your Google accounts. Each account's free Google Drive storage joins one pool, and the photos and videos you choose are backed up to whichever account has the most space.

One storage pool

Add several Google accounts; their free space adds up.

You choose what's backed up

Pick folders or single items. Nothing uploads unless you select it.

Everything in one place

Phone and Drive photos together, with albums, favourites and search.

Private by design

No servers of ours. Files go straight between your phone and your Google Drive.

Unified Gallery is a personal Android app shared with friends as an APK. It uses the Google Drive API only to show, back up and manage files in the Drive of the accounts you add.
